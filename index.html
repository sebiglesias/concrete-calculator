Frequently Asked Questions

How many 80 lb bags of concrete are in a cubic yard?

There are 45 bags of 80 lb concrete in one cubic yard (27 cubic feet / 0.60 cu ft per bag = 45 bags).

How thick should a concrete patio or slab be?

Standard residential walkways, patios, and shed slabs are poured 4 inches thick (using 2x4 lumber for formwork). For heavy pickup trucks or RV pads, a 5-inch or 6-inch reinforced pour with rebar is recommended.

Why add a 10% concrete waste buffer?

Subgrades and gravel bases are rarely perfectly level. Subgrade depressions, spills, and form deflection routinely consume 8% to 10% more volume than theoretical math estimates.
